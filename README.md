### About Me

I'm a DevOps & Cloud Engineer from Sri Lanka who can't stop building new things — from Kubernetes platforms in the cloud down to custom hardware on bare-metal microcontrollers. If it doesn't exist yet, I'd rather prototype it than wait for it.

- **Cloud & DevOps** — Running and tuning large-scale **Azure Kubernetes Service (AKS)** clusters, writing infrastructure as code with **Terraform**, and building CI/CD pipelines across **Azure** and **AWS** — with security baked in from the start.
- **IoT Platforms** — Built and operate an agri-tech IoT platform on AWS: Node.js microservices, Node-RED, MQTT via **NanoMQ**, InfluxDB and MongoDB, shipped through Jenkins across Dev, QA and Production.
- **Embedded & Hardware** — ESP32 firmware on **ESP-IDF**, an open-source [solar-powered GSM agri-sensor on STM32](https://github.com/manupawickramasinghe/stm32-solar-gsm-sensor) (firmware + hardware), and custom PCBs — written in **C, C++ and Rust**.
- **AI & Agentic** — Building **LLM-powered agents** and automations, from custom ChatGPT Telegram bots to models for data prediction, plus AI, NLP and eye-tracking on embedded devices.
- **Open Source** — Performance work on Android NVR and emulator projects, squeezing more out of low-RAM, budget hardware.
- **Homelab** — **Home Assistant** automations and **Frigate** NVR for AI-powered CCTV.

**Currently building:**

- A distributed compute cluster that turns old Android flagships and desktops into one pool for media transcoding and subtitling.
- A fully DIY Bluetooth audio "puck" on ESP32 that makes any wired headphones wireless.

**Fun fact:** I run on a steady mix of electronics tinkering and J-Rock (Base Ball Bear heavily featured on the playlist).
